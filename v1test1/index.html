What's Next

Q1 2026

Security Audit Completion

Sectora Protocol completes its third-party security audit cycle, covering smart contracts, custody logic, and the verification layer.

Q2 2026

Testnet Launch

The Sectora testnet opens to security researchers and early partners for stress-testing custody and tokenization flows.

Custody Network Genesis

Onboarding of the first wave of validators and custody nodes, moving Sectora from development to an active, distributed network.
