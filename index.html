Roadmap

From audit to mainnet.

Six milestones between the security audit and cross-chain expansion. Each one gates the next: nothing ships before the layer below it is verified.

06 milestones · Q1 2026 — Q2 2027

Q1 2026

Security audit completion

Third-party audit cycle covering smart contracts, custody logic and the verification layer.

Q2 2026

Testnet launch

The testnet opens to security researchers and early partners to stress-test custody and tokenization flows.

Q3 2026

Custody network genesis

First wave of validators and custody nodes, moving from development to an active distributed network.

Q4 2026

Mainnet launch

Verifiable tokenization and institutional-grade custody in production. Supply reduces to 25,000,000.

Q1 2027

Institutional integrations

First integrations with custody partners and exchanges, expanding access to tokenized real-world assets.

Q2 2027

Cross-chain expansion

Verification and custody coverage extended across additional chains, under the same audited model.
